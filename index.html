01 / ABOUT

Technology should make business simpler, not harder.

We build digital products that connect customer experience, operations and data into systems people can actually use.

CM Technologies is based in Mbombela and works with businesses that need more than a pretty interface. We focus on useful digital infrastructure: customer-facing websites, mobile apps, internal systems, client portals and automation that solve real operational problems.

Every build starts with the business problem. We then design the experience, engineer the platform and connect the pieces needed to make it work reliably across desktop and mobile.
